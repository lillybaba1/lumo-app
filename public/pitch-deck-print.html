The Jula people have been merchants along the trans-Saharan trade routes for centuries. Their name means "trader" in Mandinka. JulaZone honors this legacy while modernizing it for the digital age—connecting Hamburg's quality standards with West African consumers through technology built by someone who understands both worlds.
