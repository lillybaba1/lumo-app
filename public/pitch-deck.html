Market Strategy

Two strategic entry points into the African market

🇬🇲 Banjul

The Gateway

A stable, manageable market to refine our logistics and quality control operations before scaling.

  • Stable regulatory environment
  • Strong diaspora connections to Europe
  • Ideal for operations testing
  • Lower customer acquisition costs
  • Quality control refinement

🇳🇬 Lagos

The Giant

The heart of African e-commerce and tech growth, providing the volume necessary for rapid scale.

  • 200M+ population market
  • Africa's largest e-commerce market
  • Tech-savvy consumer base
  • Established payment infrastructure
  • High growth potential

Solving the "Trust Gap"

JulaZone ensures European standards of quality control from our Hamburg hub, delivered through a platform built specifically for the African mobile-first consumer. We bridge the gap that has historically prevented seamless trade between Europe and Africa.
